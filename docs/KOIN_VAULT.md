# KOIN Vault

OURO's Connect menu offers KOIN Vault alongside Kondor and Aurvania login.
Scan the locally generated QR from the wallet's Connect button, or open the
wallet link on the same device. Approve the connection with your passkey.
Keep KOIN Vault open to review and approve marketplace transactions.

Buying, listing (including batches), cancelling, and wallet-signed minting
use the Vault relay and its mana sponsor. OURO sends only contract-call
operations; Vault builds, signs after user approval, broadcasts, and confirms
the transaction. OURO does not receive a wallet key or use its ordinary
secp256k1 sponsorship endpoint for these accounts. Sessions are kept in the
current tab's sessionStorage and checked on reload. Disconnect revokes them.

Free collection launches and free server mints retain their existing flow.
Paid collection launches require Kondor or an Aurvania account because their
co-signed contract-upload transaction is outside the current Vault protocol.

## Deployment

Deploy the KOIN Vault allowlist update before deploying OURO. If the wallet
host explicitly sets DAPP_ORIGINS, include all four origins:

```
DAPP_ORIGINS=https://trade.koinoskit.site,https://app.tradekoinos.com,https://ouro.lifestyle,https://www.ouro.lifestyle
```

The wallet URL remains https://wallet.usekoinos.com. No OURO environment
variables, private keys, or build step are needed. QR codes are generated by
the vendored MIT-licensed qrcode-generator 1.4.4; no external QR service
receives the pairing secret. Test a fresh connection and a small transaction
on a real device after both deployments.
